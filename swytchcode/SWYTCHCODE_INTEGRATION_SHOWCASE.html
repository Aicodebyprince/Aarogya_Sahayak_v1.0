Aarogya Sahayak uses Swytchcode as its enterprise-grade AI execution & governance layer. Every external tool call—from Sarvam AI Indic voice translation in Marathi and Hindi to emergency ASHA notifications—routes through Swytchcode for strict schema enforcement, sliding-window idempotency, and real-time observability.
